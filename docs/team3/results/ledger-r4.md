## Recommendation
Only **R4-01 (`stopZ` 100, stop off)** passes the keep rule: Momentum +$86.7 (bootstrap share 0.973, CI [−0.04, 6.36] $/day, both halves > 0), Core +$133.8 same sign, and on Momentum the trades the baseline stopped lose less when held (matched mean −42.9 → −31.7 bps). It is a small effect with a CI touching 0, and on Core the formerly stopped trades lose *more* (−42.7 → −49.2) and the p5 trade worsens (−55 → −68), so the gain there comes from changed paths, not from the stopped trades recovering. It also removes the M1 entry cap (3 extra |z| ≥ 3.5 entries per universe); a combining-step variant that keeps the cap with no stop would need its own row. The partway target (R4-02) and scale-out (R4-03) both move the mechanism as predicted (win rate up, avg win down) but net does not hold: a partway exit grosses only ~13 bps on Momentum and ~3–4 bps on Core, below the ~15 bps round trip, as the research note warned (no edge-gate fix). Candidate for the combining step: R4-01 only. No promotion: the lead decides.

**VERSION bump needed:** yes. R4 adds trading logic behind two default-off switches (`usePartwayTarget`, `useScaleOut`, knob `tpFraction`) and a new exit reason `partway`; with both off the results match A03/A04 exactly (R4-V1…V4). R4-01 itself needs no code (a config value).
